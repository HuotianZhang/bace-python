1BACE console — three directions, three artboards each

One decision separates the three: what the home surface is. Everything the brief fixes is shared — the pinned rail with relay first and bias LIVE as an alert, inheritance drawn rather than typed, three counters at three time scales, and every trace plotted from the measured files (220 K transient, five J-V curves from −4 V, Q per loop, the 9-temperature Q(T) column, the real 21:04 → 01:26 wall clock).

The vocabulary is symbols, not sentences. Words are spent on values; state is a glyph, a rule or a fill. σ_Q = 0.000000 draws hollow with a dashed whisker (not recorded); intensity stays blank in mW cm⁻² (not calibrated).
