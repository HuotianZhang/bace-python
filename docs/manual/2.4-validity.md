# Validity

The conditions under which Q is complete and n = Q/(eAd) is a bulk density, and the checks that show it.

## Completeness

Q against V_coll saturates; Q at two delays agrees; Q against window length.

## Bulk density

Thickness, intensity and injection barriers; the contact space-charge regions.

## Absolute calibration

A known capacitor stepped by a known voltage: Q = C ΔV.
