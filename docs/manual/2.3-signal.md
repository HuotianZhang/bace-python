# Measurement chain

The measurement chain from the device current to Q, and what each stage contributes to the error.

## Current conversion and digitiser

The sample-holder amplifier delivers the device current to CHAN2 as a voltage V_s. The chain's conversion factor is R_s = 5.192 V/A, written as an equivalent resistance of 5.192 Ω (`rig.toml [electrical] sense_resistor_ohm`); it belongs to the present amplifier, not to a resistor in series with the device (1.2, "Open points"). The driver converts:

    I(t) = current_sign · V_s(t) / R_s

Q is proportional to 1/R_s, so an error in R_s is the same relative error in Q. The capacitor calibration of 2.4 measures it. `current_sign` = −1 is a convention of the chain, not physics: it sets the sign written down and changes nothing at the device.

| Stage | Behaviour | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Vertical range | Fitted to the light trace by auto-range; the shot's dark trace uses the same range | Both traces share one zero offset, which cancels in light − dark |
| Zero offset | About 0.16 mA at the range of a typical run, different at each range | Cancels in light − dark; seen as the common level before the trigger (1.4) |
| Resolution | 1.5–1.6 µA per sample at a 4.5–11.4 mA peak | `analysis/2026-09-06/` |
| Clipping | A trace beyond the range holds the extreme value | Q of that shot is meaningless; the rail check fails it (4.2) |
| Sag after the step | About 0.14 mA, also with the device disconnected | Common to both traces; cancels |

Sources: `analysis/2026-10-26/scope-window/` unless stated.

## Averaging

Each trace is the average of N_avg acquisitions, one per LED cycle: at 500 Hz, 200 averages take 0.4 s. Noise that is independent between cycles falls as 1/√N_avg. A change that is common to every cycle of one trace, such as a drift of the amplifier, is not reduced.

The digitiser's averager is cleared before every acquisition, and the number of acquisitions is read back after it. A trace with fewer than N_avg acquisitions would be a partial average and stops the run. An averager that is not cleared mixes the previous trace into the next and makes Q too small.

A run repeats the shot over n_loops loops. With σ_loop the standard deviation of Q per loop, the run summary reports the mean of Q with its standard error σ_loop/√n over the n completed loops (1.4); the run file stores σ_loop (`charge/std`).

| Setting | σ_loop | Time per shot | Time for a 1 % standard error at one point |
|---|---|---|---|
| N_avg 200, settle 0.5 / 0.5 / 1 s | 0.009 nC | 6.3 s | 1.9 min |
| N_avg 200, settle 0.1 / 0.1 / 0.2 s | 0.012 nC | 3.9 s | 2.3 min |
| N_avg 200, settle 2 / 2 / 5 s | 0.021 nC | 17.3 s | 33 min |
| N_avg 50, settle 0.5 / 0.5 / 1 s | 0.029 nC | 5.0 s | 16 min |

\|Q\| ≈ 0.21 nC, 6 µs window; settle times are `settle_s` / `dark_settle_s` / `shutter_settle_s`. Source: `analysis/2026-10-26/scope-window/`, `numbers-settle.txt`. The means of the four settings agree within their errors. Longer settle times increase σ_loop, because the drift between the two traces grows with the time between them (next sections).

## Baseline

Before integration, a constant I_0 is subtracted from light − dark. With `offset_region = "pre-trigger"` it is the mean of light − dark before the trigger, ending 10 ns before it to exclude the Sync edge's pickup.

| Region | Before the trigger | Last 10 % of the record |
|---|---|---|
| What it contains | Neither trace has seen the step; only the zero offsets, which are equal | The end of the extraction current, when the tail is slow |
| Effect on Q | None, when the traces do not drift | Q 10–20 % too small on a 2 µs record (`analysis/2026-10-26/scope-window/`); on a longer record, the drift at the end of the record enters Q |
| Depends on the record length | No | Yes |

A baseline taken at the end of the record forces the integrated charge to level off there whether the extraction is over or not, so it cannot reveal an incomplete window.

## Drift between the two traces

The two traces of a shot are taken seconds apart. A constant offset that changes between them is removed by the baseline. A change of the chain's response after the step, relative to its level before the trigger, is not: it appears in light − dark as a late current.

A null run tests this: the shutter is shut for both traces and `dark_reference = "same"`, so the two traces differ in nothing. The late level of light − dark minus its level before the trigger:

| Run | Mean ± standard deviation per shot | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Null | −1.2 ± 2.9 µA | `analysis/2026-10-26/scope-window/`, `numbers-stats.txt` |
| BACE, same device | −1.3 ± 2.9 µA | same |

The two agree, so the late offset in a BACE run is drift of the chain, not device current. Reverse leakage is ruled out separately: it changes by 0.19 µA between V_coll and V_coll − V_oc, with the opposite sign.

A drift current σ_I integrated over a window of width w gives

    σ_Q ≈ σ_I · w = 2.9 µA × 6 µs ≈ 0.017 nC per shot

which is the size of the observed σ_loop at 6 µs, 0.008–0.017 nC. Two measures reduce its effect:

| Measure | Effect |
|---|---|
| `alternate_order = true` | The dark trace comes first on even loops, which reverses the sign of a stable drift's contribution. The mean over loop pairs cancels it to the extent the drift is the same in both orders. |
| Short settle times | Less time between the traces, less drift (previous section) |

## Uncertainty budget

For one point of \|Q\| ≈ 0.2 nC with a 6 µs window and n_loops loops.

| Contribution | Size | Kind | Reduced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drift between the traces | 0.017 nC per shot | Random | √n_loops |
| Mean drift −1.2 µA × 6 µs | 0.007 nC, about 3.5 % | Systematic | `alternate_order`, over an even number of loops |
| Charge after the window | 4 % relative to a 15 µs window, on the slowest device measured | Systematic, \|Q\| too small | A wider window, at the cost of noise (2.2) |
| Timing error between the traces | Not quantified | Systematic when present | The timing check fails the shot (4.2) |
| Leakage difference | 0.19 µA × 6 µs ≈ 0.001 nC, under 1 % | Systematic | — |
| The device's C(V) over the two step ranges | Not measured | Systematic | A C(V) measurement of the device over both ranges |
| R_s | Not measured | Systematic, scales Q | A calibration (2.4) |
| Random noise within a trace | Part of σ_loop; at N_avg = 50, σ_loop is three times that at 200 | Random | N_avg, loops |

Sources: `analysis/2026-10-26/scope-window/` and `analysis/2026-09-06/`. Report Q as the mean over the loops with its standard error, and state the window and the number of loops.
