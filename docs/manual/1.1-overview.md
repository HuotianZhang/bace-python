# What the bench measures

BACE in one page: what is held, what is switched, what is integrated, and what the number means.

## The measurement

Steady illumination at V_pre = V_oc; light off and bias stepped to V_coll; the extraction current integrated.

## The result

Q, and the carrier density n = Q/(eAd) where that holds (2.4).

## What the bench adds

J-V, V_oc and J_sc, intensity series, temperature series.
