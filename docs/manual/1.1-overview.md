# Measurement overview

The bench measures the charge stored in a thin-film solar cell under steady illumination at open circuit. It holds the cell at its open-circuit voltage while it is illuminated, switches the light off, steps the bias to a reverse voltage that extracts the stored carriers, and integrates the resulting current. The method is bias-assisted charge extraction (BACE); its physics is in Part II.

## The measurement

One LED cycle, repeated at 500 Hz:

| Phase | Light | Bias at the device | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Illumination, 1 ms | on | V_pre, normally V_oc | Generation and recombination reach a steady state; no current flows at V_oc. |
| Step | off | V_pre → V_coll | The field reverses and drives the stored carriers to the contacts. |
| Extraction, 10 µs | off | V_coll | The digitiser records the current through the sense resistor. |
| Rest | off | V_pre | The device returns to the prebias for the next cycle. |

The digitiser averages N_avg such cycles into one light trace. A dark trace is recorded with the shutter shut, for a step of the same height that starts at 0 V instead of V_pre (the dark reference, 2.1). The difference removes the current that charges the device's geometric capacitance, and the charge follows as

    Q = ∫ (I_light − I_dark − I_0) dt

where I_0 is the baseline, the mean of light − dark before the trigger. The integral runs over the integration window, which starts 2 ns before the field reaches the device and lasts 6 µs (2.2). One light trace and one dark trace make a shot; a run repeats the shot over n_loops loops and reports the mean of Q with its standard error.

## The result

| Quantity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Q | The excess charge extracted from the steady state at V_oc, relative to the device in the dark at 0 V (2.1). |
| n = \|Q\|/(eAd) | The mean carrier density, where A is the device area and d the active-layer thickness. It is a bulk density only under the conditions of 2.4. |

Repeating the measurement over the LED level gives n against the generation rate, and from it the recombination order and coefficient. Repeating it over temperature gives their temperature dependence.

## What else the bench measures

| Module | Function |
|---|---|
| `jv` | Current–voltage curves in the dark or under light, with the Keithley 2400. |
| `jv_bace` | A J–V curve at the LED level of a BACE run, which also gives the V_oc that run is centred on. |
| `light` | Sets the shutter position and the LED output. |
| `power` | The light power on the Newport 1918-C. |
| `temperature` | A cryostat setpoint on the Lake Shore 331, held until the reading is stable. |

A pipeline combines these, for example a BACE measurement at each of several LED levels at each of several temperatures.
